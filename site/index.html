A few more details

Does the app clean my disk automatically?

No. You choose the items and confirm every removal, with separate actions for Trash and permanent deletion. AI recommendations never run cleanup. A cleanup candidate is not a guarantee that an entire folder is safe to delete.

Do I need to connect an AI provider?

No. Scanning, browsing sizes, revealing items in Finder and exporting work locally. AI is only used for extra recommendations when you choose to request them.

Why might sizes differ from Finder?

Disk Manage counts allocated blocks. APFS clones, snapshots, compression and inaccessible directories affect the result. A folder's size does not guarantee how much space deleting it will free.

What has been verified in this build?

Tests and the release build passed. Both architectures were checked in the package, and the app window was launched on Apple Silicon. Intel runtime, Developer ID signing and notarization have not been verified. See the release for details and the archive's SHA-256 checksum.
